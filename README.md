## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **149,116 coffee shop transaction records** using PostgreSQL to evaluate sales performance, product demand, store performance, and revenue trends.

The primary objective is to transform transactional data into actionable business insights using SQL.

The project includes **25 business-focused SQL queries**, covering fundamental SQL operations, aggregations, subqueries, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), window functions, ranking, and time-series analysis.

The analysis identifies key revenue drivers, compares store performance, evaluates product categories, and measures monthly sales growth.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project aims to answer the following business questions:

1. What is the total revenue generated by the coffee shop?
2. How many transactions and product units were recorded?
3. Which store generates the highest revenue?
4. Which store has the highest average transaction value?
5. Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
6. What are the top-performing products?
7. How does revenue change month over month?
8. Which month recorded the highest sales?
9. What are the busiest hours and days?
10. How can SQL window functions support business performance analysis?

# 📈 Business Analysis & Key Insights

## 1. Overall Sales Performance

The first stage of the analysis focused on calculating key business performance indicators.

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Transactions | 149,116 |
| Total Units Sold | 214,470 |
| Total Revenue | $698,812.33 |
| Average Transaction Value | $4.69 |
| Average Units per Transaction | 1.44 |

### SQL Query

```sql
SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS total_transactions,
    SUM(transaction_qty) AS total_units_sold,
    ROUND(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS total_revenue,
    ROUND(AVG(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS avg_transaction_value
FROM coffee_sales;
```

### Business Insights

- The coffee shop generated approximately **$698.8K in total revenue**.
- A total of **214,470 units** were sold across **149,116 transactions**.
- The average transaction generated approximately **$4.69 in revenue**.
- Customers purchased approximately **1.44 units per transaction**.

### Business Interpretation

The average transaction value provides a baseline for evaluating store-level sales performance.

Increasing transaction volume or average transaction value could contribute to future revenue growth.

## 2. Store Performance Analysis

The second stage compared revenue, transaction volume, and units sold across three store locations.

### Store Revenue Comparison

| Store Location | Transactions | Units Sold | Revenue | Average Transaction Value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hell's Kitchen | 50,735 | 71,737 | $236,511.17 | $4.66 |
| Astoria | 50,599 | 70,991 | $232,243.91 | $4.59 |
| Lower Manhattan | 47,782 | 71,742 | $230,057.25 | $4.81 |

### SQL Query

```sql
SELECT
    store_location,
    COUNT(*) AS total_transactions,
    SUM(transaction_qty) AS total_units_sold,
    ROUND(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS total_revenue,
    ROUND(AVG(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS avg_transaction_value
FROM coffee_sales
GROUP BY store_location
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
```

### Key Findings

**1. Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue.**

- Total Revenue: **$236,511.17**
- Revenue Contribution: **33.85%**
- Total Transactions: **50,735**

**2. Lower Manhattan recorded the highest average transaction value.**

- Average Transaction Value: **$4.81**
- Total Revenue: **$230,057.25**

Despite recording fewer transactions than the other locations, Lower Manhattan achieved the highest revenue per transaction.

**3. Revenue was relatively balanced across all locations.**

| Store | Revenue Contribution |
|---|---:|
| Hell's Kitchen | 33.85% |
| Astoria | 33.23% |
| Lower Manhattan | 32.92% |

### Business Recommendations

- Investigate the product mix contributing to Hell's Kitchen's strong revenue performance.
- Analyze the factors behind Lower Manhattan's higher average transaction value.
- Compare product demand across stores to identify opportunities for improving sales.
- Use store-level sales trends to support inventory and staffing decisions.

## 3. Product Category Performance Analysis

The third stage identified the categories contributing the most revenue.

### Revenue by Product Category

| Product Category | Units Sold | Total Revenue | Revenue Contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Coffee | 89,250 | $269,952.45 | 38.63% |
| Tea | 69,737 | $196,405.95 | 28.11% |
| Bakery | 23,214 | $82,315.64 | 11.78% |
| Drinking Chocolate | 17,457 | $72,416.00 | 10.36% |
| Coffee Beans | 1,828 | $40,085.25 | 5.74% |
| Branded | 776 | $13,607.00 | 1.95% |
| Loose Tea | 1,210 | $11,213.60 | 1.60% |
| Flavours | 10,511 | $8,408.80 | 1.20% |
| Packaged Chocolate | 487 | $4,407.64 | 0.63% |

### SQL Query

```sql
SELECT
    product_category,
    COUNT(*) AS total_transactions,
    SUM(transaction_qty) AS total_units_sold,
    ROUND(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS total_revenue,
    ROUND(
        100.0 * SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price)
        / SUM(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price)) OVER (),
        2
    ) AS revenue_contribution_pct
FROM coffee_sales
GROUP BY product_category
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
```

### Key Findings

**1. Coffee is the primary revenue driver.**

Coffee generated **$269,952.45**, contributing **38.63% of total revenue**.

**2. Coffee and Tea dominate overall sales.**

Combined revenue:

**$466,358.40**

Combined revenue contribution:

**66.74%**

Approximately two-thirds of total revenue came from Coffee and Tea.

**3. Bakery is the third-largest revenue category.**

Bakery generated **$82,315.64**, accounting for **11.78% of total revenue**.

**4. Coffee Beans have relatively high revenue per unit.**

Coffee Beans generated **$40,085.25** from only **1,828 units sold**.

Average revenue per unit:

**Approximately $21.93**

### Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory availability for Coffee and Tea.
- Explore opportunities to promote bakery products alongside beverages.
- Evaluate Coffee Beans as a higher-priced product category.
- Review lower-revenue categories to identify opportunities for improving performance.

## 4. Monthly Revenue Growth Analysis

The fourth stage evaluated revenue trends and month-over-month growth from January to June 2023.

### Monthly Sales Performance

| Month | Transactions | Units Sold | Revenue | MoM Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| January 2023 | 17,314 | 24,870 | $81,677.74 | — |
| February 2023 | 16,359 | 23,550 | $76,145.19 | -6.77% |
| March 2023 | 21,229 | 30,406 | $98,834.68 | +29.80% |
| April 2023 | 25,335 | 36,469 | $118,941.08 | +20.34% |
| May 2023 | 33,527 | 48,233 | $156,727.76 | +31.77% |
| June 2023 | 35,352 | 50,942 | $166,485.88 | +6.23% |

### SQL Query

```sql
WITH monthly_sales AS (
    SELECT
        DATE_TRUNC('month', transaction_date)::DATE AS month,
        COUNT(*) AS total_transactions,
        SUM(transaction_qty) AS total_units_sold,
        SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price) AS revenue
    FROM coffee_sales
    GROUP BY 1
),

monthly_growth AS (
    SELECT
        month,
        total_transactions,
        total_units_sold,
        revenue,
        LAG(revenue) OVER (
            ORDER BY month
        ) AS previous_month_revenue
    FROM monthly_sales
)

SELECT
    TO_CHAR(month, 'Mon YYYY') AS month,
    total_transactions,
    total_units_sold,
    ROUND(revenue, 2) AS monthly_revenue,
    ROUND(previous_month_revenue, 2) AS previous_month_revenue,
    ROUND(
        100.0 * (revenue - previous_month_revenue)
        / NULLIF(previous_month_revenue, 0),
        2
    ) AS mom_growth_percentage
FROM monthly_growth
ORDER BY monthly_growth.month;
```

### Key Findings

**1. June recorded the highest monthly revenue.**

- Revenue: **$166,485.88**
- Transactions: **35,352**
- Units Sold: **50,942**

**2. May recorded the highest month-over-month growth.**

May revenue increased by **31.77%** compared with April.

**3. February experienced a revenue decline.**

Revenue declined by **6.77%** compared with January.

**4. Revenue more than doubled between January and June.**

January Revenue: **$81,677.74**

June Revenue: **$166,485.88**

Overall Increase: **Approximately 103.83%**

**5. Transaction volume also increased significantly.**

Transactions increased from **17,314 in January** to **35,352 in June**.

This represents approximately **104.18% growth**.

### Business Recommendations

- Investigate the factors contributing to strong revenue growth during March, April, and May.
- Examine February's performance using daily sales averages.
- Evaluate staffing requirements during periods of increasing transaction volume.
- Monitor transaction volume and average transaction value to understand revenue growth drivers.
